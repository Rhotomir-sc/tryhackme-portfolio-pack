# Learning Journal

This folder contains short reflections from my TryHackMe learning process.

The goal of this section is to document what I learned, what confused me, what I improved, and how my understanding changed during hands-on cybersecurity practice.

---

## Purpose

This folder answers one main question:

> What did I learn from my lab practice over time?

This section may include:

- Short learning reflections
- Lab-related notes
- Confusing concepts
- Improvements after mistakes
- Connections between TryHackMe labs, Google Cybersecurity topics, and school coursework

---

## Journal Entry Format

Each journal entry should include:

- Date
- Topic or lab name
- What I practiced
- What I understood
- What confused me
- What I improved
- Short reflection
